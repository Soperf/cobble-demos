Welcome to our home

We are James & Eleanor Hartley. When we found Foxglove House, nestled gently against the fells with Windermere shimmering in the distance, we knew we had to share it. We’ve spent the last three years restoring its Edwardian charm, creating a peaceful haven for walkers, dreamers, and those seeking a quieter pace of life.

Our philosophy is simple: warm hospitality, comfortable beds, and a breakfast worth waking up for. We source our sausages from the local butcher in Ambleside, bake our own sourdough daily, and gather eggs from our own hens. Whether you’re setting out for a challenging hike or a gentle stroll to the lake, your day starts right here.
