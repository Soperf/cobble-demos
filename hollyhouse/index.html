A Peaceful Adult Haven in England's Favourite Village

Welcome to Holly House, where genuine hospitality, historic honey limestone charm, and contemporary boutique comfort come together seamlessly.

After a day wandering the charming low bridges of Bourton-on-the-Water or walking the scenic trail to Lower Slaughter, return to a peaceful guest lounge and tranquil private garden.

Every room is individually styled with handmade king-size beds, luxury memory foam mattresses, silent in-room refrigerators, and Duck Island botanical toiletries. Here, there are no noisy tour groups—just thoughtful personal service and total countryside calm.

Award-Winning Breakfast Cooked Fresh to Order

Proud recipients of 10 consecutive Tourist Board Breakfast Awards, breakfast at Holly House is a celebrated countryside ritual.

Served daily from 8:30am to 9:30am in our sunny garden-view conservatory, our extensive menu highlights the finest artisan produce from Gloucestershire and Cotswold farm shops.

Whether you choose our classic full English with Gloucester Old Spot sausages and dry-cured bacon, or lighter options such as smoked salmon with scrambled eggs, fresh berry compotes, and homemade preserves, every dish is prepared fresh just for you.

Finding Holly House

Location & Sat-Nav Guidance

Situated on Station Road in Bourton-on-the-Water, just 350 yards from the village green and river bridges.

⚠️ Sat-Nav Important Tip: Please ensure your navigation is specifically set to Bourton-on-the-Water. The postcode GL54 2ER can occasionally direct drivers towards Cheltenham if the village name is omitted.
🚗
By Car:

Easily accessed via the A429 (Fosse Way) or A40. Drive straight onto our private courtyard for your guaranteed reserved parking space.

🚆
By Train & Bus:

Nearest rail station is Moreton-in-Marsh (GWR direct from London Paddington, 90 mins). Regular local buses and taxis connect directly to Bourton-on-the-Water.

🥾
Walks from the Door:

Level footpaths lead directly from Holly House to Lower Slaughter (25 mins), Upper Slaughter, and along the scenic Windrush Way.
