Our Story · Shaun & Gemma Dickens

Casual Riverside Dining,
Crafted with Passion & Warmth

“Making the most of our beautiful riverside setting, we bring warmth and colour to honest dishes we love in a relaxed, comfortable atmosphere — food to be enjoyed with family and friends alike.”

Bistro at The Boathouse is an independent, family-run riverside bistro perched right on the River Thames in Henley. Led by Shaun and Gemma Dickens, our philosophy is simple: top-tier local provenance, warm hospitality, and an understated ambience where everyone feels at home. Whether it is a morning coffee and fresh pastry on the deck, a relaxed brunch with loved ones, or an evening of fresh Cornish mussels and steak frites watching the boats glide by, we take pride in every dish we serve.

Shaun & Gemma Dickens

Head Chef & Proprietors

Hospitality & Setting · Riverside Pillars

4 Reasons to Gather at The Boathouse

An authentic taste of Henley living by the tranquil waters of the River Thames

01

Unrivalled River Deck

Our open-air heated deck directly overlooks the River Thames, offering breath-taking views of swans, weeping willows, and rowing crews.

02

Gabriel Machin & South Coast

Handcrafted bangers from Henley's premier butcher Gabriel Machin, and day-boat Cornish mussels steamed fresh with garlic cream.

03

All-Day Casual Bistro

From morning Boathouse Bonanza breakfasts to candlelit dry-aged steak frites and chilled European wines as dusk settles.

04

Dog-Friendly Riverside Deck

While our indoor dining room remains calm and tranquil, well-behaved dogs are warmly welcomed on our spacious outside deck.
