## The Solution: A Bespoke, Direct-Conversion Website

We have developed a complete, high-converting digital redesign specifically engineered for Woodbine House:

1. **Instant 1-Click Room Enquiry:** Guests browsing your 4 rooms can click "Enquire This Room", which instantly smooth-scrolls and pre-selects their chosen room in the enquiry engine.
2. **The Ferry Traveller Magnet:** Uig is the ferry portal to Harris and North Uist. Our design prominently highlights your **"Ferry-Friendly Early Breakfast Guarantee"** — a feature OTAs cannot highlight, giving travellers an irresistible reason to book direct.
3. **Highland Heritage Storytelling:** Highlighting your status as Uig's longest-running B&B, your *Secret Scotland Recommended* status, *Scotland's Best B&Bs* certification, and Tripadvisor 5-star reviews.
4. **Sub-Second Mobile Performance:** Lightweight single-file architecture with zero bloated JavaScript or slow plugins, ensuring instant loading for travellers road-tripping Skye on 4G connections.
5. **Anti-Spam Shield & Personalised Confirmation:** Integrated invisible honeypot to stop automated spam bots, with instant confirmation generation (`WB-2026-XXXX`).
