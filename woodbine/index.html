Our Story & Hospitality

Warm Highland Welcomes Since 2017

Perched gracefully on the hillside overlooking the tranquil waters of Uig Bay, Woodbine House was originally built in 1890 as a Victorian residence, now lovingly updated to blend period character with modern cosy comfort.

When we moved to Skye in 2017, our ambition was simple: to create a restful, unhurried haven for travellers exploring the wild beauty of Trotternish. Officially recognised by Secret Scotland and proud members of Scotland's Best B&Bs, we ensure every detail of your Highland stay is tailored with genuine warmth.

Whether you are catching the early morning CalMac ferry to the Western Isles, hiking the ancient Quiraing ridges, or seeking solitude by our evening peat fire, Steve & Cass are here to ensure your Scottish adventure is truly memorable.

Explore The Trotternish Peninsula

The Ideal Base For Skye's Wonders

Woodbine House sits peacefully at the head of Uig Bay, positioned moments from world-renowned geological wonders and the CalMac Ferry Terminal (Uig Pier) to the Outer Hebrides.

15-Min Walk · 3-Min Drive

The Fairy Glen

An enchanting miniature landscape of cone-shaped grassy mounds, ancient rowan trees, and Castle Ewen ruin.

15-Min Scenic Drive

The Quiraing

Scotland's most dramatic landslip plateau. A breathtaking circuit walk past the Needle, Table, and Prison peaks.

25-Min Coastal Drive

Old Man of Storr

The iconic basalt rock pinnacle towering above the Sound of Raasay. A classic Skye sunrise photography summit.

5-Min Walk Downhill

Uig Pier (CalMac Terminal)

Direct daily sailings to Tarbert (Isle of Harris) and Lochmaddy (North Uist). Early breakfast available for ferry travellers.

Arrival By Car

Follow the scenic A87 north from Portree (15 miles) to postal code IV51 9XP. Navigation what3words: ///respect.caused.middle. Turn uphill directly before the pier; our private guest car park is immediately on the right.

Public Transport & Bus

CityLink coaches connect Glasgow, Fort William, and Inverness directly to Uig Pier, just a brief 5-minute stroll from Woodbine House.

Ferry Connections

We gladly welcome guests travelling via Caledonian MacBrayne ferries to/from the Western Isles, with luggage drop-off readily arranged.
